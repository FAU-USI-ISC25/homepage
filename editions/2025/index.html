Welcome to FAUSION

We are the FAUSION Student Cluster Competition Team, representing Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg and Università della Svizzera Italiana at ISC'25 Hamburg in the Student Cluster Competition.

Our team consists of six students from the Friedrich-Alexander-Universität (FAU) and the Università della Svizzera Italiana (USI). FAU master student Jonah Holtmann and two doctoral students, Aditya Ujeniya (NHR@FAU) and Daniel Vega (USI), supervise the ISC SCC team.
